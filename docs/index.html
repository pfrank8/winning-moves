Winning Moves

Made for Garrett, who is going to be better at this than the person who built it.

Game theory is the mathematics of choices that depend on other people's choices. Chess is a game. So is splitting a cookie, bidding at an auction, choosing which road to drive on, and deciding whether to trust someone who might not trust you back.

This site has 21 chapters in four parts. Every chapter teaches one real idea, the way a mathematician would say it, and gives you a game to play against Robo, a robot that has read every game theory book ever written and never makes a mistake unless you ask it to. Do something genuinely clever in a chapter and you earn a star. Nobody can earn a star by clicking around.

Start at Chapter 1 if you are new. Jump anywhere if you are not. The Big words page collects every term, and the Arcade collects every game.
